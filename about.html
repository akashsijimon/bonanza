About Bonanza Houseboats

Bonanza Houseboats is an Alappuzha-based houseboat operator focused on safe, comfortable, and authentic Kerala backwater travel. We serve couples, families, and group travelers looking for premium houseboat booking in Alleppey and Kuttanad.

What We Offer

- Day cruise and overnight houseboat packages in Alappuzha.

- Standard, premium, and group-friendly houseboat options.

- Onboard meals inspired by local Kerala cuisine.

- WhatsApp-first support for quick booking and custom plans.

Why Guests Choose Us

Our team combines local route knowledge, well-maintained boats, and transparent pricing to help guests enjoy memorable Kerala backwater cruises. We prioritize safety, hygiene, and service quality from check-in to checkout.
