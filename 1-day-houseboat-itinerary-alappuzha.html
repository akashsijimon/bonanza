1 Day Houseboat Itinerary in Alappuzha

A one day houseboat trip in Alappuzha is ideal for travelers who want a complete Kerala backwater experience in limited time.

Sample Itinerary

12:00 PM: Check-in and welcome drink.

1:00 PM: Cruise through narrow canals and paddy field routes.

2:00 PM: Kerala lunch onboard with local fish curry and vegetarian options.

4:30 PM: Tea and snacks near sunset viewpoints.

5:30 PM: Return to boarding point.

Tips for Better Experience

Book weekday slots for quieter routes, carry light cotton wear, and pre-confirm meal preferences before departure.
