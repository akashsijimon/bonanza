Alappuzha Houseboat Prices Guide

Houseboat pricing in Alappuzha depends on room count, cruise type, onboard comfort level, and travel season.

What Impacts Price

Boat category: Standard, premium, deluxe, or luxury group boats.

Duration: Day cruise, overnight stay, or extended package.

Season: Peak months generally cost more than off-season dates.

Inclusions: Meals, AC timing, and route customization can affect total fare.

Money-Saving Booking Tips

Travel on weekdays, share group boats where possible, and confirm package inclusions in writing before payment.
