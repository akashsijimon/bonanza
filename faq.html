Frequently Asked Questions

How can I book a houseboat in Alappuzha?

You can book directly from our home page using the WhatsApp booking form. Share your travel date, guest count, and preferred package to get quick confirmation.

What are your common cruise options?

We provide day cruises, night stays, and overnight houseboat packages across Alappuzha and Kuttanad backwaters.

Are meals included in the package?

Yes, traditional Kerala meals are available in most packages. Custom food requests can be discussed before travel.

Is the houseboat safe for families and kids?

Yes. Our boats follow safety protocols and are operated by experienced crews. Life jackets and emergency support are available onboard.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellation and refund details are listed on our Terms and Conditions page. Please contact us early for date changes or rebooking support.
