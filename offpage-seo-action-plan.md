# Off-Page SEO Action Plan for Bonanza Houseboats

## 1) Collect real reviews and publish them

- Ask every guest for a review within 24 hours of trip completion.
- Send one short WhatsApp message with direct Google review link.
- Target: 5 new reviews per week for 8 weeks.
- Add only verified reviews on-site with name, city, month, and trip type.

Template message:

`Thank you for choosing Bonanza Houseboats. If you enjoyed your trip, please leave us a quick Google review: <YOUR_GOOGLE_REVIEW_LINK>. Your feedback helps other travelers find us.`

## 2) Google Business Profile workflow

- Update profile categories (Houseboat tour agency / Travel agency).
- Add latest photos weekly (boats, food, guests, sunrise routes).
- Post weekly update with one target keyword.
- Reply to every review with local keywords naturally (Alappuzha, Kerala backwaters).

## 3) Backlink building targets (first 60 days)

- 20 travel directories/listings.
- 10 Kerala tourism blogs (guest post or feature request).
- 10 local business citations (same Name, Address, Phone format).
- 5 partner links (hotels, taxi operators, activity providers).

## 4) Suggested backlink anchor text mix

- Brand anchors: `Bonanza Houseboats`, `Bonanza Tours`.
- Partial match anchors: `Alappuzha houseboat booking`, `Kerala backwater cruise`.
- Generic anchors: `visit website`, `learn more`.

Do not overuse exact-match anchors.
