Best Season to Visit Kerala Backwaters

The best season for Kerala backwater cruises is usually from October to March, when the climate is pleasant and ideal for sightseeing.

Season Comparison

October to March: Cool weather, peak travel season, best for family trips and photography.

April to May: Warmer conditions, lower crowd, better deal availability.

June to September: Monsoon beauty, lush landscapes, occasional route limitations due to rain.

When to Book

For festive dates and holidays, reserve 2 to 4 weeks in advance. For weekdays and off-season trips, shorter notice often works.
